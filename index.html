About Us

Jeremy, the owner of a Yakiniku restaurant with deep expertise in premium beef cuts, brings a unique perspective to the concept. His partnership with Chef Nick Yoon began unexpectedly—Jeremy was introduced to Nick through a friend, and they first worked together on menu development for Jeremy's Yakiniku restaurant. During that time, Jeremy was impressed by Nick's creativity and truly enjoyed the collaboration.

As their teamwork grew, it became clear they had something special: Jeremy excels in brand vision, design, and concept-building, while Nick specializes in fusion menu development with over 20 years of culinary experience. With complementary strengths and shared standards, they decided to move forward together and create Whisper Brunch Cafe.
