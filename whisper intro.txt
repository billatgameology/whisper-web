What started as a simple introduction through a friend quickly turned into a creative match. Jeremy and Chef Nick Yoon first collaborated on menu work for Jeremy’s Yakiniku restaurant, and Jeremy was immediately drawn to Nick’s precision, originality, and talent for fusion flavors. Over time, their strengths naturally aligned—Jeremy shaping the space and brand direction, and Nick crafting a menu with depth and personality. That shared vision became Whisper: a brunch cafe built on warmth, craftsmanship, and creativity.
